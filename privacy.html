PRIVACY

Last updated: October 30, 2026

No account, no personal information

Ned Games doesn't require an account and doesn't ask for or collect your name, email or any other personal information on this page.

Visit statistics

We use a simple visitor counter to see how many people visit. When you load a page, it records which page you viewed, the website that sent you here (if any), your browser type and a random session ID. It doesn't use cookies.

Sound setting

If you turn arcade sounds on or off, that choice is saved in your own browser so it's remembered next time. It never leaves your device.

Hosting and fonts

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Like any web host, these services may log basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the page. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and Google Privacy Policy.

The games

Each game opens on its own website, which has its own privacy policy.
